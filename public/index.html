      Under the hood MCP and Qloo calls

      This page speaks JSON-RPC to the planner's MCP server at /mcp (Streamable HTTP, spec 2025-11-25). The server calls Qloo: /search and /v2/tags to resolve your tastes, then /v2/insights once per kind of stop (things to do, restaurants, bars), the later calls within 15 km of the first stop. The things to do are the ones whose Qloo tags best echo the tags /search gave your tastes.
